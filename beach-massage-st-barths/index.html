Ocean ambiance without pretending every beach is an open treatment room. We coordinate suitable beachfront, villa or hotel settings around access, shade, privacy and weather.

What “beach massage” means here

St. Barths has beaches, villas and hotel settings with different access rules. We do not promise unrestricted public-beach setups. Instead, the team confirms whether your beachfront villa, hotel or approved private area can support a comfortable treatment.

What affects the setup

Shade, wind, weather, privacy, property permission and enough stable space for the massage table all matter. An indoor or covered backup is recommended.

Pricing

The existing St. Barths source menu does not publish a separate beach-massage price. We therefore treat beach-adjacent sessions as a request to be confirmed rather than inventing a rate.

Good areas to ask about

St. Jean, Flamands, Grand Cul-de-Sac and other beachfront properties may offer appropriate settings depending on the property and conditions.
